## Safety Rules

Do not use the public profile to claim:

- ROI promises,
- certified status,
- production client results,
- accounting correctness,
- tax or legal advice,
- trading performance,
- enterprise compliance,
- fake testimonials,
- private implementation access.

Use the profile as a trust layer, not as a hype page.
