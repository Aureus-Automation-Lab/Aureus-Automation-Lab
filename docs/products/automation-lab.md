# Aureus Automation Lab

Aureus Automation Lab builds controlled AI and n8n workflow systems for business operations.

The focus is not automation for its own sake. The focus is to make repeated business work clearer, safer, and easier to operate.

## What it helps with

- sales and lead follow-up,
- operations workflows,
- document and invoice routing,
- reporting and daily summaries,
- review queues,
- n8n workflow design,
- AI-assisted drafts and classification,
- handoff and evidence.

## How the work is designed

```text
business process
-> workflow map
-> AI-assisted step
-> human review boundary
-> validation
-> evidence
-> handoff
```

## What AI may do

AI may prepare drafts, classify records, summarize activity, extract candidate information, suggest next actions, and flag missing details.

## What humans approve

People approve external messages, sensitive decisions, client-facing content, financial handoff, workflow activation, and any action where uncertainty matters.
