# Aureus OS

Aureus OS is the operating model behind Aureus Automation Lab.

It keeps AI-assisted work scoped, reviewed, validated, evidenced, and easier to hand off.

## Why it matters

AI can produce output quickly. A company also needs ownership, review, validation, action gates, and evidence.

Without that, AI work becomes scattered drafts, unclear decisions, and fragile automation.

## What Aureus OS organizes

Aureus OS connects:

- the business goal,
- the scope and constraints,
- the AI-assisted work,
- the workflow or system being built,
- the review and approval boundary,
- validation checks,
- evidence notes,
- and handoff.

## Operating model

```text
mission / task
-> scope and constraints
-> AI-assisted work
-> validation
-> action gate
-> evidence
-> handoff
```

## Action gates

Action gates stop sensitive work before it moves forward without review.

Examples include external messages, public claims, finance handoff, production workflow activation, client-facing deliverables, or sensitive data movement.

## Evidence

Evidence means the system keeps a clear record of what was requested, what was prepared, what was checked, what was approved, what was blocked, and what the next step is.

## Handoff

The goal is that another person can understand the system later without relying on hidden memory or scattered chat history.

## Typical outputs

Aureus OS work may produce a scope document, workflow map, approval model, validation notes, evidence summary, operating notes, and next-step backlog.
