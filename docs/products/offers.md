# Aureus Offers

These are public product and service starting points for Aureus Automation Lab.

The goal is to start with a clear, reviewable business process instead of trying to automate everything at once.

## Offer menu

| Offer | Best when | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Automation Audit** | A company knows work is too manual, but not what to automate first. | Process map, risk points, automation candidates, first useful scope, and next-step recommendation. |
| **n8n Workflow Review** | Existing workflows are fragile, unclear, undocumented, or hard to trust. | Review notes, failure points, sensitive-action boundaries, and improvement plan. |
| **n8n Workflow Automation Build** | A repeated process needs reliable automation. | Workflow design, implementation direction, validation notes, approval boundaries, and handoff direction. |
| **FinEcon Pilot** | Receipts, invoices, finance, documents, or reporting work needs structure. | Reviewed finance/document workflow direction with accounting and professional review boundaries. |
| **Aureus OS Setup** | A team wants AI across real work without losing control. | Operating model for scope, ownership, review, validation, evidence, approvals, and handoff. |
| **Monthly Automation Partner** | A company needs ongoing improvement and maintenance. | Practical monthly support for automation operations, reporting, iteration, and workflow improvement. |

## How a first project starts

A good first conversation starts with one question:

> Which repeated business process is currently unclear, manual, risky, or hard to operate?

From there, Aureus can map the process, define what AI may safely help with, and decide what must stay human-approved.

## Common first targets

Good first targets include:

- lead intake and follow-up,
- invoice and receipt review,
- document routing,
- missing-data checks,
- daily or weekly reporting,
- CRM or spreadsheet updates,
- approval queues,
- handoff between people and systems.

## Safety boundary

Aureus does not present AI as an uncontrolled replacement for people. AI may prepare, classify, summarize, or suggest. People approve sensitive actions, final external communication, accounting-sensitive decisions, and production workflow activation.
