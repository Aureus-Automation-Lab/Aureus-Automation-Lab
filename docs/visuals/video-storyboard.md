## Video Prompt

```text
VIDEO PROMPT CONTRACT

Create a 4-second premium public-safe video for the GitHub profile of Róbert Kolesár / robertkolesar, Founder of Aureus Automation Lab and builder of controlled AI automation systems for business execution.

CREATIVE STRATEGY
The video must explain a controlled AI automation architecture without using private implementation details. The story is: scattered manual business process -> Aureus OS system map -> workflow governance and validation -> evidence-backed handoff.

VISUAL CONTINUITY WITH HERO IMAGE
Match the dark graphite studio, warm Aureus gold proof lights, cyan workflow routes, muted green validation accents, satin glass, and matte technical material language from the profile hero image.

TIMED SHOT LIST
0-1s: Start on scattered abstract process cards on a dark table. No readable text. A few cyan lines begin to connect them.
1-2s: The camera slowly pushes in as the cards align into a clean operating platform map with GitHub/Codex delivery, n8n workflow lane, validation lens, and evidence cards represented as abstract artifacts.
2-3s: A workflow lane moves through a transparent review gate and validation lens. A sealed private vault stays outside the public review path.
3-4s: Evidence cards assemble into a clean handoff package. Hold on a calm final frame suitable as a thumbnail.

CAMERA AND MOTION
One slow cinematic push-in with subtle parallax. No flashy transitions. No shaking, no glitch, no sci-fi overload.

SCENE AND MATERIALS
Premium European B2B editorial architecture style. Dark graphite base, glass cards, satin metal, warm gold proof lights, cyan routing lines, green validation accents.

TEXT AND LOGO RULE
No readable text inside the video. No provider logos. No badges. No fake dashboards.

HARD NEGATIVE PROMPT
No secrets, credentials, endpoints, webhook URLs, workflow IDs, POHODA internals, private screenshots, client-like data, production logs, official certification imagery, revenue metrics, customer proof, fake UI walls, humanoid robots, stock-photo people, corporate handshake, coins, trophies, scary hacker visuals, copyrighted characters, or real people.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA
The first frame works as a standalone profile thumbnail. The motion explains architecture, validation, evidence, and handoff. The video does not imply production proof or customer result.
```
