## What This Repository Does Not Claim

This profile does not claim paying-customer results, production client outcomes, production trading performance, accounting correctness, tax or legal advice, promised ROI, official security certification, or public access to private systems.

Those claims require separate evidence and cannot be inferred from source-backed architecture alone.
