# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Aureus Automation Lab welcomes respectful, evidence-based collaboration. Be
clear about assumptions, critique the work rather than the person, protect
private information, and make space for people with different levels of
experience.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, personal
attacks, deliberate disruption, publishing another person's private
information, or pressuring contributors to expose credentials, client data, or
private implementation details.

## Scope

This standard applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, and other
project spaces managed by the repository owner.

## Reporting And Enforcement

Do not report conduct concerns containing sensitive details in a public issue.
Contact the repository owner through an existing private contact route shown
on the GitHub profile. The owner may edit or remove content, close threads, or
restrict participation when needed to protect contributors and the project.

Reports will be reviewed in context. No public response-time or resolution
guarantee is implied.
