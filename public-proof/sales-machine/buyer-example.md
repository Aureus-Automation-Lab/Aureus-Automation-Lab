# Sales Machine Buyer Example

This is a fictional example. It does not describe a real company, real people, real leads, or real results.

## Scenario

A local service company receives website inquiries, but follow-ups depend on whoever remembers to check the queue.

## Before

- New inquiries arrive by form and email.
- Some leads are answered quickly.
- Some are missed.
- Follow-ups are inconsistent.
- The owner cannot easily see what happened yesterday.

## Aureus workflow

```text
new inquiry
-> lead record
-> fit check
-> outreach or reply draft
-> owner approval
-> follow-up draft
-> reply classification
-> daily summary
-> audit note
```

## After

The owner has a clearer review queue:

- which leads arrived,
- which need review,
- which drafts are ready,
- which replies look positive,
- which items are blocked,
- what should happen next.

## What AI did

AI helped draft replies, suggest lead fit, classify replies, and prepare a daily summary.

## What the human approved

The owner approved final messages, sensitive replies, and next-step decisions.

## What proof was kept

The system kept lead status, draft status, approval status, reply category, next action, and a daily audit note.

No promised result or testimonial is implied.
