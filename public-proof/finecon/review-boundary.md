# FinEcon Review Boundary

FinEcon is designed as a reviewed finance/document intelligence layer.

## What FinEcon can assist with

FinEcon can assist with document intake, invoice field extraction, document classification, missing-data detection, exception queues, finance summaries, review notes, and preparation for downstream handoff.

## What must be reviewed by a person

A person should review uncertain extracted fields, missing fields, exceptions, supplier or payment-sensitive records, final summaries, and downstream handoff decisions.

## What needs professional review where required

An accountant or qualified professional should review where required:

- accounting classification,
- tax interpretation,
- legal interpretation,
- compliance-sensitive decisions,
- final import or filing decisions,
- any decision that affects official accounting records.

## What FinEcon does not claim

FinEcon does not claim accounting correctness, tax or legal advice, no-review accounting automation, replacement of accountant review, promised financial outcomes, or production readiness for every company.

## What stays private

Real invoices, accounting context, private records, credentials, endpoints, private workflow details, logs, and private screenshots stay private.
