# FinEcon Review Boundary

FinEcon is designed as a reviewed finance/document intelligence layer.

## What FinEcon can assist with

FinEcon can assist with:

- document intake,
- receipt and invoice organization,
- supplier invoice review preparation,
- issued invoice review preparation,
- income and cost document preparation,
- candidate field extraction,
- document classification,
- missing-data detection,
- exception queues,
- finance summaries,
- review notes,
- preparation for downstream handoff.

## Simple and double-entry accounting context

FinEcon can support workflows that prepare documents for simple accounting or double-entry accounting review.

This means FinEcon can organize and prepare data before review. It does not make final accounting decisions by itself.

## What must be reviewed by a person

A person should review:

- uncertain extracted fields,
- missing fields,
- exceptions,
- supplier or payment-sensitive records,
- document categories,
- final summaries,
- downstream handoff decisions.

## What needs professional review where required

An accountant or qualified professional should review where required:

- accounting classification,
- tax interpretation,
- legal interpretation,
- compliance-sensitive decisions,
- final import or filing decisions,
- any decision that affects official accounting records.

## What FinEcon does not claim

FinEcon does not claim:

- accounting correctness,
- tax or legal advice,
- no-review accounting automation,
- replacement of accountant review,
- promised financial outcomes,
- production readiness for every company.

## What stays private

Real invoices, accounting context, private records, credentials, private connection details, private workflow details, logs, and private screenshots stay private.
