# FinEcon Buyer Example

This is a fictional example. It does not describe a real company, real invoices, real financial records, or real results.

## Scenario

A small agency has receipts, supplier invoices, issued invoices, and supporting documents spread across email, shared folders, and spreadsheets.

The owner wants better visibility before documents are handed to an accounting application or accountant workflow.

## Before

- Receipts and invoices arrive in different places.
- Some files are renamed manually.
- Due dates are checked late.
- Missing fields are noticed after review starts.
- The owner has no simple summary of what needs attention.
- Handoff to the accountant is unclear.

## FinEcon workflow

```text
document arrives
-> candidate fields extracted
-> required fields checked
-> exceptions separated
-> owner review
-> finance summary
-> reviewed handoff
```

## After

The owner has a clearer review queue:

- which documents are ready,
- which documents are missing information,
- which items need human review,
- what may need professional accounting review,
- what can be summarized safely,
- what can be prepared for downstream handoff.

## What AI did

AI helped extract candidate fields, detect missing information, classify document type, and draft a summary.

## What the human reviewed

The owner reviewed exceptions, uncertain fields, document categories, and downstream handoff decisions.

Professional review remains required where accounting, tax, legal, or official-record interpretation is involved.

## What proof was kept

The system kept document status, extracted candidate fields, validation notes, exception status, review status, and handoff notes.

No accounting correctness, ROI, or customer result is implied.
