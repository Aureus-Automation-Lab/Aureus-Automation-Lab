# Aureus OS Action Gates

Action gates are review points before sensitive work moves forward.

## What Is An Action Gate?

An action gate is a deliberate stop.

It asks:

```text
Should this action happen now?
Who approves it?
What evidence exists?
What could go wrong?
```

## Actions That Need Approval

Examples:

- external email send,
- public claim,
- financial/accounting handoff,
- production workflow activation,
- client-facing deliverable,
- sensitive data movement,
- change to a live automation.

## Actions AI May Prepare But Not Execute Blindly

AI may prepare:

- an email draft,
- a LinkedIn post draft,
- a finance summary,
- a workflow change note,
- a report,
- a handoff checklist,
- a client-facing explanation.

AI should not blindly execute these when the action affects a person, client, record, payment, public claim, or production system.

## Why This Matters

Action gates keep useful AI assistance from becoming uncontrolled automation.

They help a company move faster while still knowing:

- what was prepared,
- what was reviewed,
- who approved it,
- what remains risky.

## What Evidence Is Kept

Evidence can include:

- task scope,
- draft output,
- validation notes,
- reviewer decision,
- approval status,
- blocked reason,
- next action,
- handoff note.
